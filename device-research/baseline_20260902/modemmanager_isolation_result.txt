Boot-time ModemManager isolation experiment, 2026-09-02

Precondition:
  ModemManager was enabled and active before the experiment.
  Commercial-Reliance was set as pending and activated.
  active=Reliance, pending=none, ims/imsp/imsa present.

Isolation:
  ModemManager was disabled before whole-device reboot.
  After physical replug and fastboot continue:
    ModemManager enabled=disabled
    ModemManager active=inactive
    active=Commercial-SRLTE-SS-CT
    pending=none
    ims/imsp/imsa absent

Conclusion:
  Debian ModemManager is not the component restoring CT. Restoration happens
  in the modem/MPSS boot path, EFS/NV defaults, or another pre-userspace
  platform selector.

Rollback performed:
  ModemManager was re-enabled and started.
  Final state: enabled, active.

Additional partition evidence:
  Full modemst1, modemst2, fsg and fsc hashes were identical between:
    1. Reliance active before a whole-device reboot
    2. CT active after the whole-device reboot
  Their original valid-length prefixes also match the pre-flash backup exactly.
  Therefore the tested PDC Set Selected/Activate state is not persisted in
  these raw eMMC partition bytes.
